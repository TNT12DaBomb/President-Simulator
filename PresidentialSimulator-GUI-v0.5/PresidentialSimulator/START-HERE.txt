PRESIDENTIAL SIMULATOR — DEBATE & INTERFACE EDITION v0.5

1. Extract the entire ZIP.
2. Install Java 17 or newer if it is not already installed.
3. Windows: double-click run-windows.bat.
   Linux/macOS: run sh run.sh, or open the JAR with Java.
4. Click Start a new career. How to play is available in the left sidebar.

Menu > Save / Load provides manual slots and autosave recovery.
Close the window and choose Save and exit to keep your progress.

No extra GUI libraries are needed. See README.md for limitations and controls.

Click Map, hover a state, then click it to choose campaign actions.
New decisions open automatically. Errors appear at the top of the window.

After inauguration, open Office for Cabinet, Public requests and Midterms.
Debate responses move between positions. Read their text and costs before choosing.
Blue = your statewide lead; red = opponent; gold = selected state.

Debates: preparation > answer > confidence > challenge > recovery > fact check.
Your statements remain in History. Menus pause clocks; Settings can disable them.
This release starts a new save format. Previous-version saves are not supported.
