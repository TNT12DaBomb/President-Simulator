- Retained end-to-end career, promise carryover, original campaign events, term limits, autosave/manual slots and GUI-independent commands.
- Introduced governance save format and distinct default/slot names. Monthly-v1 and quarterly-v1 career saves require the prior releases; campaign-event saves remain importable.
