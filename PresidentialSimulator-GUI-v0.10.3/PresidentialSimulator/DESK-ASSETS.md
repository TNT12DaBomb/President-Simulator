# Current desk assets

Original Java2D artwork is in DeskHub.paintAsset. The campaign surface uses plain muted wood; the presidential surface adds darker wood, green leather and gold trim. The plaque is fictional artwork, not an official seal.

Asset keys: phone, phone-red, map, folder, calendar, book, news, laptop, clipboard, letters, tray and seal. Rendering receives independent stack/attention state. DeskHub.Assets is the replacement hook. Positions are defined separately in DeskDefinition.

Current previews: docs/campaign-desk.png, docs/oval-desk.png and docs/newspaper-workspace.png. The game renders vector artwork at runtime and does not depend on preview PNGs.
